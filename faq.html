Frequently Asked Questions

How do I filter projects by category or keyword?

Click a category filter button or type a keyword into the search box on the Projects page.

Does my theme choice stay saved across pages?

Yes, your theme preference is saved in browser storage and carried across pages on the same origin.

How did the team collaborate on this website?

We used Git branches, commits, pulls, and co-author trailers to share and review changes.

How do I get in touch with the team?

Use the form on the Contact page to send us a message.
